IV · Method & lattice

How the cast is weighed

Natori fuses two instruments: a predictive grammar (X-dates → Y interval → operations → Z-dates) and cyclical calendrics (the cycle lattice). The fusion is the scoring — and the lens is switchable.

Scoring — what each lens rewards

Same traits, different weights. The Chronology lens favours the documented record; the Numbers lens favours numeral resonance.

Echo & the novel filter

A projection landing back on one of your input anchors (±1 day) is an ECHO — a confirmation, not a discovery. It still scores, but it is dimmed and tagged, and excluded from convergence. ✦ NOVEL ONLY hides echoes entirely.

19 sits in the resonance set by request — it is not in the stock MSRF table. The MSRF set itself is the six four-digit multiples of 99 in the 2178 → 6534 reverse-and-subtract orbit.

The recursion, noted not asserted

Natori is a worldbuilding and study instrument rendering a deep-time cyclical chronology as living predictive calendrics. The šar-as-turning reckoning, Annus Mundi dating and the cycle lattice are presented as that thesis, not as established science. Deep-past conversions are cycle-true rather than historically observed.

This build carries no eclipse tables. Where an earlier build scored coincidence with a real solar or lunar eclipse, that trait is simply absent here rather than approximated — a missing trait is honest; a fabricated one is not.
